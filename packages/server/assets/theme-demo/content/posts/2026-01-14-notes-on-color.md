Contrast matters more than hue. Text should reach a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background.

| Use | Light mode | Dark mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body text | `#1f2328` | `#e6edf3` |
| Muted text | `#59636e` | `#9198a1` |
| Links | `#0969da` | `#4493f8` |

Keep one accent color and use it sparingly, for links and the main action on a page.
