We arrived on a Monday with no plan beyond breakfast.

### Monday

Char kway teow from a stall that has been on the same corner for forty years.

### Wednesday

A walk through George Town: shophouses, murals, and a temple courtyard that was cool even at noon.

### Friday

The ferry back to the mainland, watching the island get smaller.
